03 / MODEL CONTRACT

Fitted. Not certified.

Finite calibration.

Each of four maturities has seven nodes at k = −0.30, −0.20, …, 0.30. Synthetic IV becomes target total variance w = σ²T. For every fixed (m,s,ρ), centered linear least squares fits a and b. This is a bounded finite-candidate minimum, not a continuous global optimum or a neural network.

Interpolation, not repair.

Between 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 years, total variance is linear in time at fixed k. The k derivatives use the same linear weights. No extrapolation is offered. The grid has 15 maturities and 61 k points from −0.30 to 0.30. Finer candidate search can improve fit while making sampled shape defects worse.

Two separate shape checks.

Calendar checks look for decreasing w at fixed log-forward moneyness. Butterfly diagnostics sample g(k) = (1 − kw′/(2w))² − (w′²/4)(1/w + 1/4) + w″/2. The continuous no-arbitrage conditions require more than sampled nonnegative values. Global wing limits, market frictions and executable spreads are not checked.

Only a numerical lesson.
